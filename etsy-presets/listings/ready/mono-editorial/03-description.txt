MONO EDITORIAL - 10 black and white Lightroom presets

Desaturating a photo does not make it black and white, it makes it grey.
Real mono conversion decides how bright each original colour becomes.
These 10 presets do that properly, so skies go dramatic and skin stays
alive.

THE PRESETS
01 Clean Mono - neutral and honest
02 Soft Portrait - low contrast, bright skin
03 Hard Light - high contrast, deep blacks
04 Matte Grey - faded gallery print flatness
05 Dark Sky - drops blue hard for dramatic skies
06 Street Grain - documentary look, coarse grain
07 Warm Sepia - brown shadows, cream highlights

WHY THESE ARE DIFFERENT
Each preset sets its own black and white channel mix, so two photos of
different subjects do not come out looking like the same grey soup.

HOW TO INSTALL
Download, unzip, open Lightroom. Desktop: File > Import Profiles and
Presets. Phone: Presets > three dots > Import Presets. Full guide is in
the download.

NOTE
Mono rewards contrast. If a photo looks flat, try Hard Light or add a
little contrast after applying.
