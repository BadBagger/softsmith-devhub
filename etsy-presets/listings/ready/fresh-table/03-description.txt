FRESH TABLE - 10 Lightroom presets for food, drinks and products

Food photography fails in specific ways. Greens go yellow and look
wilted. Whites go cream so the plate looks dirty. Reds blow out so meat
and tomato lose their texture. These 10 presets are built around those
exact problems.

  Lightroom Classic, and Photoshop Camera Raw
- Works on RAW and JPEG, including phone photos
- Step by step install guide for phone and computer
- Instant download, yours forever

THE PRESETS
01 Clean Plate - neutral and appetising, the everyday one
02 Warm Rustic - warm wood and linen, the homemade look
03 Bright Airy - white plates and lots of light, cafe menu clean
04 Dark Moody - deep surround, food carved out by light
05 Fresh Greens - salads and herbs read crisp, not wilted
06 Golden Bake - bread and pastry, crust goes golden not orange
07 Crisp Product - neutral product shot, clean whites, sharp edges
08 Matte Editorial - muted magazine finish
09 Cool Marble - for marble and ceramic, stops white going cream
10 Rich Drama - the hero shot, deep contrast and full colour

WHO THIS IS FOR
Food bloggers, recipe creators, restaurants and cafes photographing
their own menu, small food businesses, and anyone selling a physical
product who needs shots that look consistent.

WHY THESE ARE DIFFERENT
The green and yellow channels are handled separately, which is what
keeps herbs and salad looking fresh instead of tired. Whites are held
neutral so plates and marble do not drift warm.

HOW TO INSTALL
Download, unzip, open Lightroom. Desktop: File > Import Profiles and
Presets. Phone: Presets > three dots > Import Presets. Full guide is in
the download.

NOTE
A preset is a starting point. Restaurant lighting in particular varies
wildly, so expect to set white balance first, then apply.
