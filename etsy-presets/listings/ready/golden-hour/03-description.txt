WHY THESE ARE DIFFERENT
Most presets force a fixed white balance, which is why they look great on
the seller's photo and wrong on yours. These shift your photo's own white
balance instead, so they hold up across different cameras and lighting.

HOW TO INSTALL
Download, unzip, and open Lightroom. On desktop: File > Import Profiles
and Presets. On phone: Presets > three dots > Import Presets. The full
guide with screenshots-level detail is in the download.

NOTE
Presets are a starting point, not a one click finish. Every photo is lit
differently, so expect to nudge exposure after applying. That is normal
and takes seconds.
