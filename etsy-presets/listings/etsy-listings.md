## Shop setup notes

**Listing type:** Digital. Upload the matching zip from `dist/zips/`.

**Images per listing:** Etsy allows 10. Use this order:

1. `covers/<pack>-01-cover.png` - the title card
2. `covers/<pack>-03-before-after.jpg` - split before/after
3. `covers/<pack>-04-before-after.jpg` - second before/after
4. `covers/<pack>-05-grid.jpg` - four presets on one photo
5. `covers/<pack>-02-whats-included.png` - the contents card
6. A phone screenshot of the presets installed in Lightroom Mobile
7. A before/after on one of your own photos
8. An "installs in 60 seconds" graphic or screenshot of the import screen

Images 2 to 5 are generated by `make_before_after.py` from freely licensed
photos in `sources/`. Licensing is recorded in `sources/CREDITS.md`.

**Re-export images 2 to 5 from Lightroom before publishing.** They are
currently rendered by `render_preview.py`, which approximates Camera Raw
rather than being it. Open the files in `sources/`, apply the same presets
in Lightroom, export at 2000x2000, and swap them in. A listing whose
"after" does not match what the buyer gets is how you earn refund requests.

Images 6 and 7 still need you: a phone screenshot, and at least one photo
of your own so the listing is not entirely stock.

**Do not** use photos of clients without permission, and be careful with
stock photos containing recognisable people — they carry no model release,
and a product listing is commercial use.

**Pricing strategy**

- Single pack: 12.00 USD, run at 9.00 as an intro sale.
- Bundle: 29.00 USD, run at 24.00. Etsy shoppers respond to a visible
  percentage off, so set the real price and use a shop sale rather than
  just listing a low number.
- Do not go below about 7.00. Preset packs priced at 2 or 3 dollars read
  as stolen or AI-dumped and attract refund requests.

**Attributes to set**

- Digital download, instant
- Craft type: Photography
- Occasion: leave blank
- Holiday: leave blank

**What to say in the FAQ section**

- "Does this work with the free Lightroom app?" Yes, the free mobile app
  can import .xmp presets.
- "Will it work on my camera?" Yes, any camera, and phone photos too.
- "Do I need a subscription?" No.
- "Can I use these for client work?" Yes, see the licence in the download.

**Policy reminder**

Digital items are exempt from Etsy's returns requirement, but you must
still say so in the listing. The copy above already does.
