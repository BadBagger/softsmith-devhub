WHAT THESE WORK WITH

  Photoshop Elements         no, it cannot import .xmp presets
  VSCO / Snapseed / other    no, these are Lightroom presets

  RAW files and JPEGs both work. RAW has more room to move, so the
  same preset will usually look stronger and cleaner on a RAW file.

THREE THINGS WORTH KNOWING
  1. A preset is a starting point, not a one click finish. Every
     photo is lit differently. Expect to nudge Exposure and White
     Balance after applying. That is normal and takes seconds.

  2. These presets deliberately do not force a fixed white balance.
     They shift your photo's own white balance instead, so they hold
     up across different cameras and lighting.

  3. If a look is too strong, most Lightroom versions have an Amount
     slider when you hover a preset. Pull it back to taste.
